## App description

KAELIS is an experimental visual instrument built around the idea of light, reflection, depth, and motion.

What began as a kaleidoscope project gradually evolved into a layered optical engine with several visual philosophies: classic mirrored symmetry, premium crystal optics, experimental artifact states, animated menu atmospheres, and music-reactive visual exploration.

KAELIS is not only a viewer. It is a small laboratory of light: a place where images, music, crystal forms, reflections, and procedural motion meet in real time.
